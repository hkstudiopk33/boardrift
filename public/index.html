The six games

Chess — 2-player and 4-player

Chess is a strategy game in which you win by checkmating the opposing king. Two-player mode follows the full rules, including castling, en passant, promotion and every draw rule. Four-player mode is a free-for-all on a 14×14 cross-shaped board: a player who is checkmated or stalemated is out, and the last player standing wins.

How the chess AI works: an alpha-beta search engine with iterative deepening, a transposition table, quiescence search and null-move pruning. On Hard it typically looks 10–14 half-moves ahead.

Checkers — 2-player and 4-player

Checkers is played on the dark squares with diagonal moves; you win by capturing or blocking every enemy piece. Two-player mode uses American checkers rules with mandatory jumps, multi-jumps and kings. Four-player mode puts 12 men in each arm of a cross-shaped board; each player races diagonally toward the opposite side and the last player with pieces wins.

How the checkers AI works: in two-player games, alpha-beta search 10–16 moves deep; in four-player games, it weighs captures against how exposed its pieces become to every opponent.

Go on a 9×9 board

Go is an ancient territory game: players place stones, capture groups by surrounding them, and the side controlling more of the board wins. Boardrift uses area scoring with 7.5 komi, enforces ko and suicide rules, and removes dead stones automatically at the end.

How the Go AI works: Monte Carlo tree search with RAVE, simulating tens of thousands of games per move. It is a solid opponent for beginners and casual players; very strong players will beat it.

Wallvane — the race-and-block game (2, 3 or 4 players)

Wallvane is Boardrift's own race-and-block strategy game: each player races a pawn across a 9×9 board while placing walls to lengthen everyone else's route. On each turn you either move one square or place a wall; a wall may never cut a player off completely. Walls are shared out by player count — 10 each with two players, 7 with three and 5 with four.

How the Wallvane AI works: it computes every player's shortest route, searches three moves ahead in two-player games, and in bigger games drops walls on whoever is about to win.

Chinese Checkers (2, 3, 4 or 6 players)

Chinese checkers is a race on a six-pointed star: you move all 10 marbles into the opposite point by stepping or chaining jumps over other marbles. Despite its name, the game was invented in Germany in 1892 as "Stern-Halma"; the name "Chinese checkers" came from the Pressman brothers' US marketing in 1928.

How the Chinese checkers AI works: a pruned look-ahead search in two-player games, and a two-move plan that avoids leaving stragglers in bigger games.

Backgammon

Backgammon is a dice-and-strategy race in which each player moves 15 checkers around 24 points and bears them off; the first to bear off all 15 wins. Boardrift includes hitting, the bar, bearing off, the rule that both dice must be used when possible, and gammons.

How the backgammon AI works: a 2-ply expectimax search that averages each candidate move over all 21 distinct dice rolls. The dice are random for every player.

Ways to play

  • Against the AI — four difficulty levels in every game, from forgiving to Grandmaster.
  • With friends on one device — set any seat to Human and pass the phone or share the screen.
  • Online with friends — create a private room, share the 5-letter code or invite link, and fill empty seats with AI.
  • Taunts and music — the AI trash-talks (switch it off any time), players can send quick taunts, and the background music is composed live in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

Is Boardrift free to play?

Yes. All six games are free, with no account, no download and no in-game purchases. Open the page in any modern browser and start playing.

Can I play 4-player chess or 4-player checkers?

Yes. Choose "4 players" in Chess or Checkers to play free-for-all on a 14×14 cross-shaped board. Each seat can be a person on the same device, an online friend, or the AI.

How do I play online with friends?

Press "Play online with friends", enter your name and create a room. Share the 5-letter room code or the invite link; friends join with the code, take an open seat, and you press New game. Empty seats can be filled by the AI.

Which games support more than two players?

Chess and Checkers support 2 or 4 players, Wallvane supports 2, 3 or 4, and Chinese Checkers supports 2, 3, 4 or 6. Go and Backgammon are two-player games.

What is Wallvane?

Wallvane is a race-and-block strategy game made for Boardrift. You win by being first to move your pawn to the far side of a 9×9 board. Each turn you either move one square or place a wall to block others, but you may never block a pawn's path completely.

How strong is the AI?

Each game has four levels. Easy makes deliberate mistakes, Medium plays sensibly, and Hard and Grandmaster search deeply. Two-player chess and checkers on Hard search 10–16 moves ahead; Go is the easiest for experienced players to beat.

Can I turn off the AI taunts or the music?

Yes. The AI taunts, music and sound effects each have their own on/off button at the top of the page, and your choice is remembered on that device.

Is the music copyrighted?

No third-party music is used. The background music is generated live in your browser by code written for Boardrift, so there are no recordings or samples to license.

Is Chinese checkers actually from China?

No. It was invented in Germany in 1892 as "Stern-Halma", a star-shaped version of the American game Halma. The name "Chinese checkers" was a 1928 American marketing choice by Bill and Jack Pressman.

What is the most searched board game online?

Chess. As of March 2026 it was the most-searched board game on Google worldwide, with about 1.5 million searches a month, ahead of mahjong and checkers (Semrush data).
